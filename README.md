# Multi-Asset Market Risk Engine

A CAD-based market risk engine for a multi-asset portfolio of equities, US Treasuries, gold, oil and a Government of Canada bond priced off a bootstrapped zero curve. It computes 1-day 99% VaR and 97.5% Expected Shortfall using historical, parametric and Monte Carlo methods, validates VaR with Kupiec and Christoffersen backtests, and runs stress tests. Built entirely on free data (Bank of Canada, FRED, Yahoo Finance).
